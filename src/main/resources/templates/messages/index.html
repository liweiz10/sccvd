Welcome to scCVD: A comprehensive single-cell omics atlas of cerebrovascular disease.

scCVD is a manually curated, multi-omics database dedicated to Cerebrovascular Disease research, integrating single-cell transcriptomic, epigenomic and bulk transcriptomic data with standardized cell type annotation and multi-level regulatory analyses across diverse species, tissues, disease conditions and post-disease time points.

  • Multi-Omics Integration: Unifies 511 scRNA-seq, 13 scATAC-seq and 481 bulk RNA-seq samples across four species, diverse tissues and post-disease time points.
  • High-Quality Cell Type Annotation: Annotates over 3.73 million cells into 70 cell types through CellTypist-based automatic annotation refined by marker genes and manual review.
  • Regulatory Analyses: Provides DEGs, trajectories, TF regulatory networks, chromatin accessibility and bulk RNA-seq profiles for cross-dataset corroboration of single-cell findings.
